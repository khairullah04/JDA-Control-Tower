Only a file-reading tool was available (no search or directory listing), so every "not found" below means "not found at the paths I opened" and is marked Suspected where that matters.

**Top 3 risks**

1. **The status management reads is not trustworthy as a record.** Progress can be changed by anyone with the link, with no author, no history and no undo (finding 1); it defaults to green when unset (4), carries no "last updated" date (11), and two editing routes overwrite each other (9).
2. **The tool rests on one machine and unnamed owners.** No backup, restore or hosting instructions, finance data possibly in a single local file, weak shared passkeys, and no stated owner for the Firebase projects, service account, API key or Power Automate feed (findings 2, 7, 10).
3. **It does not yet answer the portfolio question at a glance.** Progress and spend live in separate sites with no link (3); the all-JDA dashboard shows no overspend, forecast or delay flags and measures against placeholder budgets (5, 6); and nothing can be exported as a management summary (8).

**What's done well**

- Invoice and payment records are traceable end to end: every Payment Table row opens to the sanctioned amounts and invoices behind it, every change is logged with who/when/old/new, deletes are restorable, imports preview first and skip duplicates, and exports re-import unchanged (`jda/web/js/ui/payments.js:47-63`, `finance-ui.js:219-230`, `finance-io.js:20-64`).
- The project page states the position in one plain sentence ("RM x over the sanctioned amount"), and the Payment Table keeps Total, Variance and a worded status pinned in view with over-sanctioned rows marked red (`jda/web/js/ui/project.js:33-36`, `jda/web/css/finance.css:45-57`).
- The pages are honest about gaps: a JDA with no report shows dashes rather than zeros, a red notice appears when invoices are not being saved to the real database, and deep links to a JDA, project, register or Payment Table can be shared (`portal/js/portal.js:27,39`, `jda/web/js/ui/finance-ui.js:23-24`, `jda/README.md:31-32`).

**Score:** 5/10 — the invoice and Payment Table side is close to daily-use quality for the person doing the bookkeeping, but as a management view it lacks a combined progress-and-spend summary, reliable flags, exports and an auditable status, and it has no backup or handover footing.
