# JDA Control Tower: consolidated review

Five reviewers each read the codebase on their own on 12 October 2026 and did not see each other's
findings. Their reviews are saved as returned: [security-engineer.md](security-engineer.md),
[software-engineer.md](software-engineer.md), [accountant.md](accountant.md),
[project-manager.md](project-manager.md), [submission-user.md](submission-user.md). Together they
raised 168 findings, merged below into 37.

References such as `SEC-5` or `ACC-12` are the row numbers in those five files (SEC security, SWE
software, ACC accountant, PM project manager, USR submission user). Open the row for the full
finding, its impact and its worked example.

**How far to trust this.** Every reviewer worked from the source only: nothing was run, no browser
was opened, and the real Firebase project, Drive folders and SAP files were not available. Several
reported that only the file-reading tool worked for them, so "not found" means "not at the paths
opened". Nobody inspected `drive-test-files/`, and `portal/_legacy/` was only partly read. I merged
and ranked the findings but did not re-verify each one against the code.

## 1. Scorecard

| Role | Score | Findings | Critical | High | Medium | Low | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Security engineer | 3 / 10 | 21 | 2 | 4 | 6 | 9 | The finance server is sound, but committed short passkeys, open sign-up, a world-writable portal database and a stored XSS let the controls around it be bypassed. |
| Software engineer | 5 / 10 | 35 | 1 | 3 | 15 | 16 | The CJI3 and Payment Table core is production-grade and tested; the portal's storage, sign-up and several silent data-loss paths are not. |
| Accountant | 6 / 10 | 38 | 0 | 9 | 19 | 10 | The arithmetic and the preservation of past rates are right; the controls around the figures are not yet strong enough for payment decisions. |
| Project manager | 5 / 10 | 26 | 2 | 8 | 13 | 3 | Close to daily-use quality for the bookkeeper; as a management view it lacks a combined picture, reliable flags, exports and an auditable status. |
| Submission user | 5 / 10 | 48 | 0 | 12 | 22 | 14 | The register is careful about figures and history, but typed work is easy to lose and several single clicks change live data with no way back. |

All five agree on what is solid: money is held as whole sen with one rounding rule and exact
arithmetic, each record keeps its own rate and tax rate, the server (not the browser) validates and
writes every finance change with its audit lines, deletes are restorable, and imports preview first.
